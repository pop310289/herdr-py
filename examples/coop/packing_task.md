Place 26 circles inside the unit square [0, 1] x [0, 1] so that no two circles overlap and every circle lies
entirely inside the square. Make the sum of the radii as large as possible.

Answer with a JSON list of 26 entries [x, y, r]: the centre and the radius of each circle, for example
[[0.1, 0.1, 0.1], [0.3, 0.1, 0.1], ...]

Circles may touch. The judge checks every rule exactly, on the numbers as written (no tolerance), and scores the
sum of the radii.
